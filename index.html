My Story

How unexpected detours built a reliable background in client support and operations.

I am currently based in Cebu, Philippines, where I was born and raised. I would describe myself as a friendly, approachable, and extroverted person who loves human connection. I stay calm under pressure, value clear communication, and I am always looking for ways to expand my skillset.

My career started during the pandemic as a Customer Service Representative. Dropped into a fast-paced, client-focused environment overnight, I quickly adapted—learning how to communicate across barriers, resolve concerns, and anchor myself under pressure.

From there, I moved into specialized roles, eventually serving as a Senior Case Manager at Airbnb. That experience truly taught me how to step back, look at the big picture, and thoughtfully mediate between guests and hosts to ensure fair, professional resolutions.

Later, I joined AnyoneHome as a Property Consultant, Property Manager, and Maintenance Coordinator. This role stretched my logistical muscles—handling property documentation, coordinating quick maintenance physical turnarounds, and problem-solving timelines simultaneously.

My path wasn’t planned from day one, but looking back, each step helped me realize what I enjoy most: supporting people, building order out of mess, and making sure backend operations run smoothly.
